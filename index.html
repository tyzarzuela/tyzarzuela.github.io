TYLER ZARZUELA / GAME DESIGNER & PROGRAMMER

Imagine. Create. Play.

Hello World! I'm Tyler, a game developer with a restless mind and a relentless drive to build, break, and innovate.

Honestly? My journey started with childhood exploits. I wasn't just playing games, I was QA testing them without permission.

On The Sims 3 for Wii, my sister and I broke the local economy through an inventory state desync: put a fish in the fridge, take it out, and the game would spawn a duplicate inside the container, giving us endless cash.

In Animal Crossing: New Horizons, I weaponized local co-op latency by grabbing items off a rotating table on the exact frame my friend turned it, tricking the game into duplicating rare loot directly into my pockets.

What started as playful mischief quickly evolved into my life's true calling: dissecting complex game logic, reverse-engineering core mechanics, and building robust, interactive experiences that bring people together.

I guess you could say I stopped breaking worlds just so I could start building better ones.
